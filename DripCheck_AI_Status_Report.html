01 Executive Summary & Product Vision

DripCheck AI turns a user's real physical wardrobe into an intelligent digital closet. Instead of generic conversational fashion advice, DripCheck works directly with the clothing pieces the user owns. Through multimodal vision recognition and styling logic, it answers the daily question: “What should I wear today?” while providing color theory, fit, and silhouette rationales.

Core Loop (MVP v1): Upload wardrobe photos → AI extracts attributes (type, color, pattern, fit, season) → User picks an occasion (*Casual, Office, Date, Party*) → AI curates a multi-piece outfit from their own closet → Explains why the outfit coordinates → User rates look (1–5 stars).

04 Key Feature Modules in Detail

👗 Digital Closet & AI Tagging

Simulates/calls multimodal vision models to detect clothing silhouettes, primary and accent colors, patterns, fit profiles, and seasonal tags. Persisted in local state with instant category filtering.

✨ Smart Stylist Recommendation

Context-aware outfit builder that cross-matches tops, bottoms, and footwear from the user's uploaded wardrobe. Includes natural language styling rationale and star feedback dispatch.

📌 Interactive Garment Pinning

Custom canvas overlay allowing users to tap on post images to anchor descriptive tags directly to garments (e.g., “Oversized linen shirt”), making inspiration instantly actionable.

🧬 Style DNA & Habit Gamification

Dynamic color distribution wheel, dominant silhouette archetype indicator, consecutive styling streaks, and unlockable achievement badges celebrating wardrobe curation milestones.
